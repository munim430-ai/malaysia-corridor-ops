# Strategic & Policy Product Grounding Rule

Whenever developing a strategic proposal, ministerial brief, or B2B pitch:
1. **Explicitly Map Architecture to Code**: For every policy pillar or operational mechanism (e.g., "Direct Sourcing", "Zero-Upfront Escrow", "Blind Medical Allocation"), identify the exact software application, repository, frontend view, and database table implementing it.
2. **Clarify the Concrete Deliverable Stack**: Always provide a clear, 3-sided breakdown of the tangible product:
   - What the End-User (Worker/Customer) sees and interacts with (Mobile App / Web UI).
   - What the Enterprise/Employer sees (B2B SaaS Portal / Compliance Dashboard).
   - What the Regulator/Admin controls (Clearinghouse Backend / API Engine).
3. **Prevent Pure Abstraction**: Never leave a proposal at the level of abstract policy without specifying the tangible software and data flow that makes the policy technically enforceable.
