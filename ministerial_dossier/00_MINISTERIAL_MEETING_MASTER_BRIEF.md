## 2. Chronological 15-Minute Meeting Gameplan

* **Goal:** Establish immediate trust and align with the Minister's personal intention to clean up corruption.
* **Opening Script:**

In the past, the government gazetted cost was Tk 78,990, but our poorest workers were forced to pay 6.5 to 7.0 lakh takas. That extra 5.5 lakh taka did not go to the government, nor did it go to Malaysian employers—it was siphoned by a 5-layer syndicate of village dalals and cartel middlemen. Today, I am here to share with you a working operational model that makes corruption structurally impossible, costs the government zero taka, and ensures workers leave with zero upfront debt."*

### Phase 2: The Core Working Model (Minutes 3:00 – 10:00)
* **Goal:** Present the 4 pillars of the Open Digital Clearinghouse (Hybrid G2B).
* **Key Talking Points:**
  1. **Direct Digital Sourcing:** Eliminate village dalals by requiring workers to register through Union Digital Centers (UDCs) and BMET mobile portals with biometric NID verification.
  2. **Blind Medical Queuing:** Eliminate medical syndicates by having software randomly allocate candidates across DGHS-accredited clinics for a fixed Tk 8,000 fee.
  3. **Zero Upfront Cash (PKB Model):** Probashi Kallyan Bank directly pays the airline ticket, medical test, and BMET smart card. The worker pays **Tk 0 out of his pocket** in Bangladesh and pays off the Tk 78,990 micro-loan through an easy RM 250/month salary deduction over 6 months in Malaysia.
  4. **Open Agency Pool:** All 1,500+ licensed BAIRA agencies participate as certified logistics fulfillment partners at a guaranteed Tk 20,000 statutory fee, breaking the 102 cartel while giving all agencies legal profit.
