* **Visual Layout:** Premium Deep Emerald & Gold background. Official seal of Bangladesh left, modern digital network graphic right.

* **Subtitle:** An Open Digital Clearinghouse Model to Eliminate Syndicates, Enforce the Tk 78,990 Ceiling, and Safeguard National Remittances.
* **Presenter Talk Track (Verbatim):**
  > *"Honorable Minister, thank you for your time today. As Bangladesh prepares to sign the upcoming bilateral Joint Working Group agreement with Malaysia, our nation stands at a critical crossroads. In the past, whenever this corridor opened, it was hijacked by a cartel of 102 agencies and village middlemen, forcing our poorest youth to pay upwards of 6 to 7 lakh takas for a job that officially costs under 80,000 takas. It uses technology to eliminate every single dalal, costs the government zero additional budget, gives all 1,500 legitimate BAIRA agencies fair business, and ensures our workers leave with zero upfront debt bondage."*

### Slide 2: The Anatomy of the 7-Lakh Extortion
* **Visual Layout:** Side-by-side comparison bar chart. Left bar: Government Gazetted Cost (Tk 78,990 / $660). Right bar: Syndicate Extortion (Tk 650,000 / $5,400). A red callout box shows "Tk 5.7 Lakh Stolen per Worker".
* **Key Data Points:**
  - Official Cost: BMET smart card (Tk 11k) + Medical (Tk 8k) + Airfare (Tk 35k) + Welfare/VDR/Insurance = Tk 78,990.
  - Actual Charged: Village Dalal (Tk 1.75L) + Sub-Agent (Tk 1.0L) + Syndicate Kickbacks (Tk 2.5L) + Malaysian Agents (Tk 1.0L) + Inflated Medicals (Tk 45k) = Tk 6,70,000.
* **Presenter Talk Track:**
  > *"Minister Sir, let us look at the raw numbers. The actual cost to process and fly a worker to Kuala Lumpur—including mandatory levy, FOMEMA, VDR, airfare, BMET smart card, and welfare fund—is only 78,990 takas. Yet, in the previous cycle, the market rate was 6.5 to 7 lakh takas. Where does that 5.7 lakh taka difference go? It is entirely consumed by a 5-layer chain of non-value-adding middlemen: village touts, sub-agents, syndicate gatekeepers, and Malaysian shell entities. If 400,000 workers go to Malaysia under this old system, over 22,000 Crore Takas is drained from rural Bangladesh into the pockets of a few dozen illicit syndicates."*

He cannot send remittances home for the first two years because every ringgit goes to loan sharks charging 30% interest in his home village. Furthermore, major Malaysian employers in electronics, rubber, and palm oil are under severe pressure from US and European buyers to enforce the ILO 'Employer-Pays Principle'. By allowing syndicates to charge workers, we are making Bangladeshi workers a toxic liability for top Malaysian employers who fear US trade bans."*

### Slide 4: The Core Solution: The "Open Digital Clearinghouse" (Hybrid G2B)
* **Visual Layout:** Clean 3-tier architecture diagram showing Direct Worker Registration ➔ Central Automated Queue ➔ Open RL Fulfillment Pool.
* **Key Pillars:**
  1. **Direct Digital Sourcing:** Workers register directly via mobile/UDC on BMET Central.
  2. **Automated Quota Matching:** Malaysian employer job orders are matched blindly to pre-screened workers.
  3. **Democratized Agency Pool:** All 1,500+ licensed RL holders participate as certified ground logistics partners at a fixed Tk 20,000 service fee.
* **Presenter Talk Track:**
  > *"We do not need to invent an entirely new bureaucracy, nor do we need to shut down the private sector. The solution is a Hybrid G2B model called the Open Digital Clearinghouse. In this model, the government controls the digital gate: worker registration and quota allocation happen on an open central platform, completely bypassing village dalals. The 1,500+ licensed BAIRA agencies are invited to participate as certified fulfillment partners. Instead of extorting workers, agencies earn a transparent, guaranteed service fee of 20,000 takas per worker for ground logistics, passport processing, and departure support. Agencies make healthy, predictable profits; workers pay zero bribes."*

### Slide 5: Pillar 1 — Dismantling Village Dalals via Direct Digital Sourcing
* **Visual Layout:** Process flowchart showing worker applying from Union Digital Center (UDC) ➔ Instant SMS/WhatsApp Verification ➔ Immutable Digital Profile.
* **Key Mechanisms:**
  - 5,000+ Union Digital Centers across all 64 districts act as localized physical registration hubs.
  - Integration with national NID and BMET database prevents duplicate or forged identity submissions.
  - No middleman can 'sell' a slot because quotas are matched directly by system algorithm based on verified trade skills.
* **Presenter Talk Track:**
  > *"How do we stop the village dalal in Comilla, Tangail, or Bogura? We make him irrelevant. Today, every union in Bangladesh has a Union Digital Center, and 90% of rural youth have access to smartphones. By mandating that all Malaysia job openings must be applied for directly through the BMET digital portal or UDC with biometric NID verification, a dalal cannot claim he has 'special ministerial quota'. The worker receives an automated SMS confirming his status, interview date, and exact official fees."*

---

### Slide 6: Pillar 2 — Centralized Blind Medical Allocation
* **Visual Layout:** Flow diagram showing Worker Registration ➔ Automated Blind Assignment ➔ Fixed BDT 8,000 Test ➔ Real-Time Digital Certificate Upload.
* **Anti-Syndicate Safeguard:**
  - In previous regimes, a syndicate of 25-100 medical centers charged Tk 45,000-50,000 per worker through fake referral fees.
  - Blind algorithm randomly distributes workers across all DGHS/BMET accredited diagnostic centers in Bangladesh.
  - Fixed statutory fee of BDT 8,000 paid exclusively via digital bank transfer.
* **Presenter Talk Track:**
  > *"The medical test has historically been the syndicate's biggest cash cow, extracting 40,000 to 50,000 takas for a basic health screening. Under our architecture, the system implements 'Blind Medical Allocation'. When a candidate is shortlisted, the software automatically and randomly assigns him to an accredited clinic 24 hours before the appointment. The clinic has zero prior knowledge of who is coming, eliminating referral kickbacks. The worker pays the gazetted fee of 8,000 takas directly via bank payment."*

### Slide 7: Pillar 3 — Zero Upfront Cash via Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB)
* **Visual Layout:** Financial loop diagram: PKB disburse BDT 78,990 ➔ Directly to BMET / Airline / Medical / RL ➔ Worker Deployed ➔ Monthly Remittance Deduct (RM 250/mo for 6 mos).
* **Key Economics:**
  - Worker pays **Tk 0 out-of-pocket** in Bangladesh.
  - Probashi Kallyan Bank (or partner MFIs like BRAC/Grameen) issues a direct collateral-free migration loan of Tk 78,990.
  - Loan is not given to the worker in cash; it is disbursed directly to vendors (Airline ticket, Medical center, BMET smart card, Agency fee).
  - Repayment: RM 250/month (~Tk 6,500) deducted through formal bank remittance from the worker's Malaysian salary over just 6 months.
* **Presenter Talk Track:**
  > *"Here is the most transformative part of this model: the worker leaves Bangladesh having paid exactly ZERO takas out of his pocket. Through Probashi Kallyan Bank and state-backed microfinance institutions, we establish a closed-loop migration facility. The bank pays the airfare, medical, and BMET fees directly to the respective service providers. The worker does not need to sell his family land or borrow from usurious moneylenders. Once he starts earning his RM 1,800 to RM 2,500 monthly salary in Malaysia, a modest deduction of RM 250 per month clears his entire loan in just 6 months. After month six, 100% of his earnings are pure profit sent home as national remittances."*
